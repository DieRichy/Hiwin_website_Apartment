01 ABOUT HIWIN

An Osaka foundation.
A wider Japan.

We connect a place to stay with the city around it.

Based in Osaka, HIWIN operates Apartment Hotel 11 (Eleven). Our group brings together property development, design, construction and operations to create apartment hotels that combine the comfort of home with the convenience of a hotel.

For travel agencies, that means a portfolio of stays for independent travellers, families and groups — with dining and local services connected through our network.
