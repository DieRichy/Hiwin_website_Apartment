Common questions

Why aren't rates shown on this site?

Partner rates depend on dates, property and group size. Send us your requirements and we will reply with a quotation and our rate sheet.

Can you accommodate groups?

Yes. Properties average around 50 to 70 rooms and some have more than 100, so we can suggest one or more properties for your group.

Do the rooms suit families?

Most featured room types sleep up to 4 guests and have a kitchen. Larger suites are available on request.

Can we book at short notice?

Yes. For urgent requests, contact us by phone, WhatsApp or LINE and we will check availability right away.

Do you have properties outside Osaka?

Yes. We also have properties in Kyoto, Tokyo, Okinawa, Kobe and Nagoya. Tell us your itinerary and we will suggest options.
